How should I store the soufflés and crème brûlée?

Place soufflés and crème brûlée in your freezer immediately.

How long will they last in my freezer?

Please refer to the Best Used by date on the box. Frozen soufflés and crème brûlée last up to 12 months.

Can I refreeze after thawing?

No. Once thawed, prepare and eat immediately or store in your refrigerator.

Refrigeration

Thawed soufflés and crème brûlée can be refrigerated up to 3 days.

Leftover soufflés

Wrap baked soufflés and refrigerate. Reheat in microwave 30s. They won't rise again, but taste remains great.

Serving suggestions

Serve with ice cream, whipped cream, powdered sugar, and berries. Open the center and add warm chocolate sauce or crème anglaise.

Are your products natural?

All–natural, trans fat free, no artificial colors or flavors.

Baking Instructions

Chocolate & Chocolate Mint: 375°F. From frozen: 22–24m. Thawed: 18–20m.

Pumpkin: 400°F. From frozen: 22–24m. Thawed: 18–22m.

Crème Brûlée: Broil 3–5m until golden; rest to harden. From thawed: broil 3–5m; rest 3–6m.
